## 1. Clearance Status & Evidence Distinctions

- **Clearance States Used:** `CLEARED` · `REPLACED — creator visual review complete; not formal legal clearance` · `NEEDS DOCUMENTATION` · `NEEDS PERMISSION` · `NEEDS REPLACEMENT` · `LEGAL REVIEW` · `UNKNOWN`
- **Critical Provenance Distinction:** A textual reference or procedural box-mesh homage to a third-party franchise does **not** mean an imported third-party 3D model is present in the repository. Each entry below explicitly distinguishes **textual references / object names**, **procedural geometry**, **2D textures**, **audio**, **video**, and **imported third-party packages/assets**.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Build Clearance Gate (For Upcoming Implementation Phases)

Before shipping any **Unity Fellowship playable build**, **public trailer**, or **commercial store page**:
1. Zero **`CRITICAL`** items (IP-01) may appear in playable scenes, HUD text, screenshots, or video.
2. All **`HIGH`** items (IP-02 through IP-07) must be either replaced with original assets or backed by documented written permission / formal legal review.
3. All **`MEDIUM`** items (IP-08 through IP-13) must have verified commercial license documentation recorded in [Third-Party Notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
