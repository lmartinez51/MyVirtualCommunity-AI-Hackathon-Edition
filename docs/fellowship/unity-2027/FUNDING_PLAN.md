## 2. Non-Financial Unity Fellowship Support Objectives (`PLANNED`)

Beyond direct financial funding, Fellowship mentorship and Unity ecosystem support directly address the project's highest-leverage technical and commercial risks:

1. **Unity 6 URP Performance & Lighting Mentorship:** Optimizing the large multi-layer `BorderCorridorScene.unity` (occlusion culling, shadow-atlas scaling, Forward+ light budgets, additive scene streaming) to maintain crisp visuals on both low-VRAM laptops and mainstream gaming PCs.
2. **UI Toolkit / UGUI Architecture Guidance:** Migrating legacy IMGUI overlays (`FirstPersonPlayer.cs`) to a modern, scalable, accessible bilingual UI architecture supporting controller/keyboard navigation.
3. **Commercial Game Design & Pacing Review:** Playtest feedback from experienced Unity mentors on environmental clue clarity, vertical-slice pacing, and balancing grounded cultural realism with supernatural mystery.
4. **Go-to-Market, Legal & Storefront Readiness:** Guidance on commercial Steam/PC launch readiness, build signing/packaging, accessibility standards, and IP-safe commercial presentation of real-world-inspired environments.
